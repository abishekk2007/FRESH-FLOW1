## 🚀 What is Fresh Flow?

Fresh Flow is an **AI-powered food waste elimination system** for Indian restaurants and grocery stores that:

1. **🤖 PREDICTS** - AI learns demand patterns and forecasts food spoilage 2-3 hours in advance (87% accuracy)
2. **💰 PRICES** - System auto-suggests dynamic discounts to maximize revenue instead of throwing away food
3. **🤝 PREVENTS** - Unsold food is automatically matched with verified NGOs via QR-tracked donations

## ✨ Core Features (This Demo)

### 1️⃣ Restaurant Manager Dashboard
- **Add food** to system with time and quantity
- **Real-time countdown timer** showing time until expiry
- **AI demand prediction** showing what percentage will sell

- **One-click approval** to apply discount live

### 2️⃣ Customer App Interface
- **Find discounts** on fresh food nearby
- **See expiry timer** (creates urgency)
- **Order now** with verified freshness proof
- **Capture revenue** that would otherwise be wasted

### 3️⃣ NGO Donation System
- **Auto-match** unsold food with verified orphanages
- **QR-tracked donations** for food safety verification
- **5-level NGO verification** (govt registered, physical visit, hygiene cert)
- **Photo proof** and temperature tracking

### 4️⃣ Analytics Dashboard
- **Real-time impact metrics**:
  - Food added vs sold vs donated
  - Revenue generated vs waste prevented
  - People fed count
  - CO2 emissions avoided

## 📋 Demo Workflow (5 Minutes)

### Step 1: Restaurant Adds Food (1 min)
1. Navigate to **"Restaurant"** tab
2. Select "Chicken Biryani"
3. Set time to "11:00"
4. Set quantity to "20"
5. Click **"Add to system"**

**What happens:**
- ✅ Countdown timer starts (5 hours to expiry)
- ✅ AI prediction shows up: "87% accuracy, 50% won't sell"
- ✅ Discount recommendation: "Apply 40% at 3 PM"

### Step 2: Manager Approves Discount (1 min)
1. Click **"Approve 40% discount now"**

**What happens:**
- ✅ Discount goes live on customer app
- ✅ System auto-matches with NGO

### Step 3: Customer Orders (1 min)
1. Go to **"Customer"** tab
2. See: "Chicken Biryani - 40% off - ₹150 (was ₹250)"
3. Click **"Order now"**

**What happens:**
- ✅ Order confirmed instantly
- ✅ Revenue captured that would be lost

### Step 4: NGO Gets Donation (1 min)
1. Go to **"NGO"** tab
2. See: "Hope House Orphanage matched, 2km away"
3. See QR code for tracking
4. Click **"Confirm donation & send to NGO"**

**What happens:**
- ✅ Donation logged with QR tracking
- ✅ Temperature, photo, delivery verified
- ✅ 5+ children will be fed

### Step 5: See Impact (1 min)
1. Go to **"Analytics"** tab
2. See real-time metrics:
   - Revenue generated: ₹3,450
